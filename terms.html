  • One 90-minute online session and pre-session review of a resume up to 4 pages.

  • No line-by-line resume rewriting, LinkedIn rewriting, or mock interview.

Executive Career Review | NT$8,800

  • One resume up to 4 pages, up to 2 target roles, and one primary-language version.
  • Up to 5 leadership achievements and one 90-minute strategy session.
  • After the main session and receipt of complete inputs, the advisor provides the first restructured resume draft.
  • The client must submit one consolidated round of feedback in a single document or email within 10 days. Late or additional revisions require separate scope, timing, and fee confirmation.
  • One revision is followed by one 45-minute review. The review covers confirmation, explanation, and necessary minor recommendations, not a new career direction, new target roles, another language, or a full rewrite.

  • One resume up to 4 pages, up to 3 target roles, and one primary-language version.
  • 3 strategy sessions, 8 Leadership Story frameworks, one 75-minute mock interview, one consolidated written response per week, and a 90-day career action roadmap.
  • Normally completed within 3–4 weeks.
